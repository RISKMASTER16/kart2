      How this works

      Wikimedia publishes English Wikipedia page views once an hour, usually one to two hours after the hour ends, so "live" here means the newest published hour. The local server fetches each new hour, keeps the top 150,000 pages, and runs the same XGBoost model and standard rule as in our results. Scores use only data that existed before each scored hour.

      This planner runs on your computer and makes no change to Wikipedia's servers. Page titles link to the articles on Wikipedia.
